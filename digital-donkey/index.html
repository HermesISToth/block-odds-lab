Terms of Service · Privacy Policy

Digital Donkey is a content operations workflow for TikTok Shop product videos. It helps the account owner and approved operators prepare product footage, review captions and disclosures, upload videos, check creator posting settings, and publish approved TikTok content from authorized creator accounts.

The TikTok developer integration is used for Login Kit authorization and Content Posting API workflows. The requested TikTok scopes support creator identity checks, video upload, direct post testing, and post status checks inside the Digital Donkey publishing process.

What the workflow does

Operators select a prepared product video, confirm the product and caption details, authorize the connected TikTok creator account, and send the approved video through TikTok's posting flow. The workflow keeps a record of file names, timestamps, captions, post status, and operational notes so each public post can be checked before and after publishing.

TikTok integration

Digital Donkey uses TikTok authorization only after the creator account owner grants access. The integration is designed for Digital Donkey's own TikTok Shop content operation and is not a public social media scheduler, data broker, or unrelated advertising tool.

TikTok Shop product cards

Product-card attachment and final preflight checks may still be completed in TikTok Studio when a product requires manual verification. The API workflow is used to reduce repetitive upload and publishing work while preserving review of product claims, captions, and disclosures.
