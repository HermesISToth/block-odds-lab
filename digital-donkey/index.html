Digital Donkey

Authorized TikTok Shop video workflow

Digital Donkey is a creator-operations workflow for the @donkey.digital TikTok account and Digital Donkey content business. It helps approved operators prepare product-focused short videos, confirm captions and disclosures, authorize TikTok access, send approved media through TikTok's posting flow, and verify publishing status.

Terms of Service · Privacy Policy · App Flow

What the service does

The workflow is built for Digital Donkey's own TikTok Shop production process. Operators review raw product footage, create or select an approved vertical video, check the product-card match, confirm caption language, and publish only after the account owner has authorized the TikTok connection.

Who uses it

Access is controlled for the Digital Donkey account owner and approved operators working on the creator account and related content campaigns. It is not a public scheduler, scraping product, data broker, advertising exchange, or consumer social network.

TikTok integration

Digital Donkey uses TikTok Login Kit and the Content Posting API for account authorization, creator identity checks, video upload or direct-post workflows, publish-status review, and operational auditing for videos the connected creator account has approved.

Review boundary

TikTok Shop product-card selection and final compliance checks may remain manual in TikTok Studio when an exact active product card must be verified. The API workflow is intended to reduce repetitive upload work without bypassing product, caption, disclosure, or platform review.

Operator workflow

  1. Choose a prepared Digital Donkey product video and confirm that the product is visible, the audio is present, and the caption is accurate.
  2. Connect the authorized TikTok creator account with TikTok Login Kit.
  3. Check creator posting settings and available privacy options before upload or direct post.
  4. Submit the approved video through TikTok's Content Posting API or continue manual product-card verification in TikTok Studio when required.
  5. Record the media file, caption, product-card notes, TikTok publish status, timestamp, and any manual review notes for auditability.

Data and retention

Digital Donkey stores only the operational information needed to run and audit the creator workflow, such as file names, captions, product-card checks, status messages, timestamps, and TikTok authorization tokens. TikTok account data is not sold, shared with unrelated advertisers, or used outside the authorized content workflow.
