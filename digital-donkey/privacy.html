Privacy Policy

Terms of Service · Digital Donkey Home · App Flow

Digital Donkey Content Tools use TikTok authorization only for account-authorized content operations such as confirming creator identity, reading creator posting settings, uploading approved videos, checking post status, and operating approved posting workflows.

TikTok data

TikTok access tokens and API responses are used only for the authorized Digital Donkey workflow. The tools do not sell TikTok account data, use it for unrelated advertising, or share it with unrelated third parties.

Operational data

Workflow logs may include file names, draft or post status, product-card checks, captions, timestamps, error messages, authorization status, and API status messages needed to operate and audit Digital Donkey TikTok Shop videos.

Retention and deletion

Operational logs are kept only as long as needed for publishing review, troubleshooting, and creator-account audit history. A connected TikTok account can revoke access through TikTok account settings. Digital Donkey will delete locally retained workflow records on request when they are no longer needed for legal, platform-compliance, or dispute-review purposes.

Third-party services

TikTok, TikTok Shop, vendors, affiliate platforms, and other services may process information under their own privacy policies.
