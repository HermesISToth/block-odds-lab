Self-custody gear

Three ELLIPAL cold wallet videos to watch before choosing self-custody gear

Mining odds get people thinking about Bitcoin. Cold wallets answer the quieter follow-up: where should keys live if you are done trusting an exchange?

This page links only to the current Digital Donkey ELLIPAL videos I could tie back to product-attached TikTok Shop posting records. No mismatched product clips, no generic wallet links.

ELLIPAL X Card

Best fit: someone who wants the smallest everyday-carry cold wallet format. ELLIPAL describes X Card as an NFC cold wallet that is slim, tap-to-sign, and battery-free.

ELLIPAL Titan Mini

Best fit: someone who wants air-gapped QR-code signing in a smaller hardware wallet. The Titan Mini sits between a card-style wallet and a larger vault-style touchscreen device.

Watch the product-attached Titan Mini video

Official ELLIPAL Titan Mini reference

ELLIPAL Titan 2.0

Best fit: someone who wants the more durable at-home cold-storage feel. ELLIPAL describes Titan 2.0 as air-gapped, with no WiFi, Bluetooth, or USB data connection, plus QR-code signing and a larger clear-signing touchscreen.

Watch the product-attached Titan 2.0 video

Official ELLIPAL Titan 2.0 reference

The simple comparison

Choose X Card if the real use case is pocket carry. Choose Titan Mini if you want a smaller air-gapped hardware wallet. Choose Titan 2.0 if you want the larger home-vault style device.

No cold wallet removes the need to protect your seed phrase. The goal is not the largest device; it is matching the device to the custody habits you will actually follow.
